How it works

From listing to keys

Data analysis

We track every listing in our patch for price drops, long time on the market and chain-free sales, to spot motivated sellers early.

Negotiation

We speak to agents and vendors directly to agree a genuine discount, and only take on deals where the numbers still work.

Due diligence

End values come from recent sold prices nearby. Every deal gets a builder's refurb quote and a full cost breakdown.

Deal pack

The deal goes to our investor group first, with a full pack. A £1,000 reservation (refunded if the deal falls through for reasons outside your control) takes it off the market; the £5,000 balance is due on exchange. No VAT, nothing from sellers.
