The tests are applied to my base figures. They aren't predictions. Each extra month costs £500 in holding costs plus about £1,020 in bridging interest.

What I'd want before committing

  • The offer accepted at or near £160,000
  • A builder's itemised quote after a viewing, including the boiler and electrics
  • The floor area confirmed, and whether the house has been extended
  • The rent checked against current lettings of 3-bed semis nearby

Main assumptions

  • Stamp duty at standard rates plus the 5% surcharge
  • Holding costs £500 a month while empty
  • Bridging: 75% of the price, 0.85% a month, 2% arrangement, 1% exit
  • Selling: 1.2% agent fee plus £1,500 legal
  • Buy-to-let: 75% loan to value, 5.75%, rent must cover interest by 125%
  • Letting: 10% management, 2 weeks' void a year, £1,650 a year other costs

All figures are before tax. On a live deal I'll re-run the numbers on your own assumptions.
