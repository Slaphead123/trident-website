Where I'm looking first

Arnold, around Daybrook and the town centre (NG5 6, NG5 7). Semis sell for a median of about £221,000 to £225,000, and around a third of houses sell for under £200,000. That gap is where a dated house bought well can leave room for the refurb, and there are over 400 house sales in two years to sell into.

Carlton and Gedling (NG4 1, NG4 3). A similar picture to Arnold, slightly cheaper to buy, with terraces around £180,000.

Netherfield (NG4 2) is the cheapest sector here, with about half of houses under £200,000. The whole ward needs a landlord licence, so I'd only buy there for a rental, at a price that works on the rent.

Woodthorpe (NG5 4) is a strong area, but few houses sell under £200,000, so it rarely fits a refurb budget.

This is my view from the data, to compare with your own research.
